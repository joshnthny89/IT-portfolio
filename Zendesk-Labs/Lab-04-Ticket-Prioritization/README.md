# 🎫 Lab 04 – Ticket Prioritization

## Objective

Learn how to assess and prioritize support tickets based on their impact, urgency, and business importance. This lab demonstrates how proper ticket prioritization helps ensure that critical issues receive prompt attention while maintaining an organized and efficient support workflow.

---

## Skills Practiced

- Ticket Prioritization
- Incident Assessment
- IT Service Management (ITSM)
- Customer Support Workflow
- Ticket Management
- Critical Thinking

---

## Tools Used

- Zendesk Support
- Zendesk Agent Workspace

---

# Lab Walkthrough

## Step 1 – Open the Incoming Ticket

Opened an existing support ticket within the Zendesk Agent Workspace and reviewed the customer's reported issue.

Before assigning a priority level, gathered the available information including:

- Customer request
- Description of the issue
- Potential business impact
- Current ticket status

## Step 2 – Assess Ticket Impact and Urgency

Evaluated the ticket by considering two key factors:

- **Impact** – How many users or business functions are affected?
- **Urgency** – How quickly does the issue need to be resolved?

Using both factors helps determine the appropriate priority level while ensuring support resources are allocated effectively.

## Step 3 – Assign the Appropriate Priority

Updated the ticket's priority based on the assessment.

Possible priority levels include:

- Low
- Normal
- High
- Urgent

The selected priority reflects the overall severity of the customer's issue and helps determine the order in which support requests should be addressed.

## What I Learned

This lab demonstrated the importance of evaluating both the urgency and business impact of a support request before assigning a priority level. Proper ticket prioritization helps support teams respond efficiently, allocate resources appropriately, and ensure that critical issues receive timely attention while maintaining an organized workflow.
